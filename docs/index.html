How the check works
Each employer's name is matched against the Home Office register of licensed sponsors (Skilled Worker route, A-rated). The whole name has to match, ignoring endings such as Ltd, PLC and UK. A match means the employer can sponsor a visa. It doesn't mean this role will. Agencies are kept apart, because an agency's licence doesn't cover the employer it recruits for.

    
What the register column says

      
Licensed sponsor
The employer is on the register.

      
No register match
The name wasn't found. It may be registered under another name.

      
Needs SC or DV clearance
Security clearance has UK residence requirements.

      
Agency, or No sponsorship
The employer isn't named, or the listing says it won't sponsor.
